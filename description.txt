agline: TableKeeper — database-enforced booking correctness, proven under real concurrency.

TableKeeper is a production-minded reservation service built by a three-agent software factory in Band Desktop. An Architect turns briefs into testable invariants, a Builder implements only production code, and an Auditor independently writes adversarial tests and verdicts. The FastAPI and SQLite system prevents double-booking with transactional write locking and a composite slot-claim primary key. It also supports capacity enforcement, IANA time zones, DST rejection, idempotent booking retries, atomic cancellation, and canonical UTC storage. Its four-process stress harness executes 240 mixed operations, reports zero integrity violations, runs offline in Docker, and includes a mutation mode that proves the audit catches failures.
